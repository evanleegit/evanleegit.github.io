This is a personal accountability exercise, and a baseline to measure my growth as a quantitative thinker. Moving forward, the goal is to consistently document my analysis of market trends, financial workflows, and data science applications. Success for this project means maintaining the discipline to code, write, and analyze regularly, creating a permanent archive of my technical progression.
